# Mystic Rose — Keycap Set Specification
**Subset Mystic Rose v1.0** | ISO-DE 75% | PBT Cherry Profile | 2026-05-20

Mystic Rose is a romantic, slightly gothic set: dusty rose-pink caps with a large black botanical tattoo-rose illustration flowing across the left side of the board, and a small group of matte-black accent caps. The mood is old-school tattoo flash meets soft vintage — elegant, a little dark, very tactile. Source files (Affinity .af + a rendered reference) are attached.

---

## 1. Cap colors

We use **two cap colors**. The hex values are our visual target — please match them as closely as your standard production allows.

| Zone | Color | Hex | Caps | Used for |
|---|---|---|---:|---|
| Bloom Rose | dusty rose-pink | `#C58A78` | 83 | the main body of the board — all letters, numbers, modifiers and navigation |
| Matte Black | warm near-black | `#221C18` | 10 | Esc, F5–F8, Entf, the four arrow keys, and the blank cap (top-right) |
| | | **Total** | **93** | |

**Both colors should have a matte finish** — the rose caps and the black accent caps alike. No gloss, no metallic, no shine; a soft matte surface is part of the look for this set.

The matte-black accent caps give the set a small dark anchor against all the rose — Esc and the function/arrow cluster read as a deliberate contrast group.

### The black rose illustration — what makes this set special

The heart of this set is a large black botanical tattoo-rose that flows across the left side of the board — over the Tab, Caps, left-Shift and left-Strg area, deliberately cropped at the left edge so it bleeds off the board. It's drawn in fine black linework, old-school tattoo-flash style. This illustration is the design feature we care about most, so we'd love the fine lines to come through cleanly.

Because the rose is dark, the legends sitting on top of it (Q, A, the `<` key, Caps, Strg, Win, Alt and a couple of others) are printed in **cream** instead of the usual dark ink, so they stay readable over the black artwork. That's intentional — please keep those particular legends light.

The artwork is supplied as vector/raster in the source file. The lines are fairly fine, so please check how they hold up at your print resolution — if the finest details don't reproduce cleanly, we're very happy to supply a simplified version. Just let us know.

## 2. Legend (printing) colors

| Color | Hex | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Ink Black | `#1A1612` | the standard legend color, on all the rose caps that are *not* covered by the black illustration |
| Cream | `#EFE7DA` | two uses: (1) the legends that sit on top of the black rose illustration (so they stay readable), and (2) all legends on the matte-black accent caps (Esc, F5–F8, Entf, arrows) |

Legends are UPPERCASE.

---

## 3. Font and layout

- Font: **Saira SemiCondensed Bold** (Open Font License), letter-spacing approx +0.08em
- The "Esc" legend and the "SUBSET" wordmark on the spacebar are set in **Saira SemiCondensed ExtraBold**, a touch wider (~+0.18em)
- PostScript name: `SairaSemiCondensed-Bold` (and `SairaSemiCondensed-ExtraBold` for Esc + wordmark)
- Layout: ISO-DE (German QWERTZ, with Ü Ö Ä ß and all 12 AltGr characters)
- All legends are already converted to vector outlines in the source file.
