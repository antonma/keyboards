# Neon Drive — Keycap Set Specification
**YogiKeys Neon Drive v1.0** | ISO-DE 75% | PBT Cherry Profile | 2026-05-20

Neon Drive is a synthwave / outrun-themed set: deep midnight indigo caps, hot magenta legends, cool cyan command labels, and reflective chrome accents. The mood is cold, nocturnal, and neon — 80s retro-futurism, the kind of look you'd find on a Miami-at-night album cover. The signature element is a magenta perspective grid on the spacebar, like a horizon receding into the distance. Source files (Affinity .af + a rendered reference) are attached.

---

## 1. Cap colors

We use **five cap colors**. The hex values are our visual target — please match them as closely as your standard production allows.

| Zone | Color | Hex | Caps | Used for |
|---|---|---|---:|---|
| Midnight | deep indigo | `#1F1638` | 35 | all letters (A–Z, Ü Ö Ä), punctuation caps |
| Nightshade | slightly warmer indigo | `#251A40` | 23 | number row, F1–F4, F9–F12, left Shift, Menu |
| Blackcurrant | almost-black navy | `#0E0820` | 23 | all modifiers and navigation (Tab, Caps, Enter, Strg, Win, Alt, AltGr, Fn, Druck, Roll, Pause, Einf, Entf, Pos1, Ende, Bild↑, Bild↓, plus the alternate modifier caps) |
| Chrome | polished silver | `#C9CFDA` | 7 | spacebar, Backspace, the four arrow keys, and the blank cap (top-right) |
| Magenta | hot magenta | `#E0399B` | 5 | Esc and F5–F8 |
| | | **Total** | **93** | |

The three dark tones (Blackcurrant, Midnight, Nightshade) are meant to read as three subtle steps of darkness — close to each other, not high-contrast. The only bright cap accents come from the chrome and the magenta caps.

### The chrome finish — what makes this set special

The seven chrome caps — the spacebar, Backspace, the four arrow keys, and the blank cap in the top-right corner — are the heart of this set. We're really hoping for a **real polished-chrome look** on these — like brushed or mirror-finish metal, the kind of cold reflective shine you'd see on 80s chrome lettering or a DeLorean. That metallic, reflective quality is the single design feature we care about most in Neon Drive; it's what makes the set feel premium rather than just printed.

The hex value `#C9CFDA` is our visual target for the base silver tone, but the *finish* matters more than the exact color — we'd love it to genuinely catch the light.

If a true mirror-chrome finish isn't something your process can do, a **metallic silver** (the same kind of metallic feel as the gold caps you showed us before, just in silver) would also be wonderful — that shimmer is what counts.

The one thing we'd ask is that these caps **not** end up as a flat, plain printed grey — for this design the metallic shine is essential. So if neither a chrome nor a metallic-silver finish is possible on your side, please tell us early and we'll find a path together rather than going with a flat print.

All other caps are matte.

## 2. Legend (printing) colors

Neon Drive uses a simple logic: **magenta is "input", cyan is "command"**. They should stay separate — magenta on the typing keys, cyan on the modifier/command keys.

| Layer | Color | Hex | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary glyph | hot magenta | `#E0399B` | the main character on every typing key — all letters, the numbers, F1–F4 and F9–F12. On letters and F-keys it's the only legend (centered); on number/punctuation keys it's the lower-left main character. Also used for the arrow glyphs and the Backspace word on the chrome caps. |
| Shift layer | dimmer magenta | `#B82E80` | the secondary (shift) character, upper-left, on the number row **and** the punctuation keys (e.g. `!` over `1`, `*` over `+`, `>` over `<`) |
| AltGr layer | dim cyan | `#1FAFC1` | the 12 AltGr characters (bottom-right on the cap) |
| Modifier words | cyan | `#34E0E0` | all modifier and navigation legends (Tab, Caps, Strg, etc.) and the Enter return symbol |
| Sunset glyphs | very dark navy | `#0E0820` | the F5–F8 labels (dark, so they read on the bright sunset caps) |
| Deep magenta | dark magenta | `#8B1F5C` | the word "Esc" on the magenta Esc cap |

> Please note: the dim-cyan AltGr characters `#1FAFC1` and the bright-cyan modifier words `#34E0E0` are **two separate colors, not one shared cyan** — the AltGr layer is intentionally a little quieter than the modifier words.

## 3. Font and layout

- Font: **Saira SemiCondensed Bold** (Open Font License)
- PostScript name: `SairaSemiCondensed-Bold`
- Layout: ISO-DE (German QWERTZ, with Ü Ö Ä ß and all 12 AltGr characters)
- All legends are already converted to vector outlines in the source file.

---

## 4. Brand detail — the spacebar horizon grid

The spacebar carries the signature motif: a magenta perspective grid (an outrun-style horizon receding into the distance), in `#E0399B`, filling the whole cap.

| Cap | Element |
|---|---|
| Spacebar | magenta perspective horizon grid, full cap |

The grid is built as fine vector lines in the source file. Because the lines are thin, please check how they hold up at your print resolution — if the finest lines don't come through cleanly, we're very happy to supply a simplified version with slightly thicker lines. Just let us know.
